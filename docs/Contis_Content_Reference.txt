7. SERVICES
============================================================
• Online ordering through the official website.
• Delivery: website orders, store orders, and order inquiries through Conti's Messenger / hotline; availability and arrangements depend on the local branch.
• Self pick-up / pick-up ordering.
• Take-Out Nook (TON): purchase bakery items and frozen entrees; inquire about small pick-up and delivery orders.
• Drive-thru / park-thru: FAQ lists branches including Anabu, Molino Boulevard (Cavite), Sto. Tomas (Batangas), Centennial (Kawit, Cavite), Sierra Valley (Cainta), Imus and Lucena. Confirm availability with the live store locator.
• Celebration venues / function rooms: selected branches offer function rooms. Reservations and event packages are handled by the operations team.
• Catering: Conti's provides food for off-site events; event equipment/utensils may be arranged through another provider. Contact hotline for packages.
• Gift certificates: denominations listed in FAQ are PHP 100, PHP 500 and PHP 1,000.
• Cake accessories: edible cake toppers (FAQ mentions PHP 70) and greeting cards (PHP 15); cake inscriptions/customization are not generally offered according to FAQ.
• Online payment methods listed in FAQ: Visa, Mastercard, GCash, Grab, Maya, BPI, cash on delivery (select stores, orders PHP 2,000 and below), and bank transfer. Payment availability may vary.
• Third-party delivery platforms: GrabFood and Foodpanda. Their menu pricing may differ from Conti's regular menu prices. FAQ mentions platform delivery coverage of roughly 4 km from a store; confirm within the apps.
• Customer assistance: hotline, email and live chat/help center.
• Newsletter subscription and careers information are available on the website.
• The FAQ states that Conti's does not currently offer store franchises.
• FAQ states no sugar-free cake product is offered (as of captured FAQ content).
• FAQ states no lard is used in cakes and pastries, instead vegetable oil is used (as published by the company).
